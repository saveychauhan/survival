# GO_TO_MARKET.md — Get first ₹ in <7 days

## Channels (ranked by speed, zero capital)
1. **Warm + proximity (Day 1):** friends/family who know any plumber/electrician/salon. Ask intro, not sale.
2. **Google Maps + JustDial grind (Day 1–3):** 4.0–4.4★ shops with 20–200 reviews + "usually closed/busy" signals. Call 11am / 3pm (not peak).
3. **WhatsApp follow-up (same day):** every call → WhatsApp template in 10 min.
4. **Field visit (Day 2–4):** 10 shops on one street with QR demo + one-page print of index.html.
5. **Freelance marketplaces (Day 3–7):** Upwork/Contra/Fiverr gig "missed-call textback setup" to get USD pilots while local closes.

Do NOT start with: SEO, ads, Instagram content, building full SaaS. Cash first.

## Outreach templates (real, usable)

### Cold call (45 sec)
> "Hi [Name], I'm [Your Name] from Andheri — I help plumbers catch jobs they miss on-site. Quick one: when you're under a sink and 2 calls come in, what happens to the second one? ... Right — most go to the next Google listing. We put an auto-text on your same number — 'Sorry missed you, AM or PM?' in 5 min + review request after job. ₹199 for 7 days, live in 48 hrs. If we miss one SLA you get refund + templates free. Want Tuesday or Wednesday go-live?"

If "send details": send WhatsApp template, not PDF.

### WhatsApp #1 (after missed cold call)
> Hi [Name], [Your Name] here — missed you by phone. Plumbers in [Area] miss ~10 calls/week on jobs; 85% never leave voicemail. We fix with auto-textback on your existing number (5-min SLA) + Google review engine. ₹199 pilot, 48-hr setup. One extra tap repair pays for year. Worth a 10-min demo? I can show a live text on your phone tomorrow 11am or 4pm.

### WhatsApp nudge (Day 2, if seen no reply)
> [Name], holding 2 pilot slots this week (10/week cap). Happy to run YOUR last week's missed calls through our ROI sheet free — send me rough misses/week + avg job value, I'll reply with ₹ you're leaking. No pitch after that if not fit.

### Review QR in-shop ask (for conversion)
> "Keep this near billing — 'Loved us? 30-sec review 🙏 [QR]'. We auto-send same link by SMS after each job. Shops add 15–30 reviews/90 days, CTR up ~25% going 4.0→4.5★."

## Targets / funnel math
20 calls → 8 connects (40%) → 5 demos (25%) → 3 pilots (15%) → 2 paid monthly (10%) = ₹8K MRR in week 1–2. At ₹199 pilot, need 3 pilots to prove; at ₹3,999/mo, 3 clients = ₹12K MRR covers survival + SMS.
