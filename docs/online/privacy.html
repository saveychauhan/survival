Privacy Policy.

Short version: we collect almost nothing, because there is nowhere to put it. No accounts, no database, no newsletter.

What stays with you

On your device only.

Game scores

Best averages, tap records and play counts live in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device. Clear site data and they are gone.

Preferences

Sound on/off and similar toggles are stored the same way — locally, only.

Third parties

Who else is in the room.

Ads & affiliate links

Pages load ads from Adsterra (and reserved slots for JuicyAds) and link to Amazon. Those companies may set their own cookies and log views/clicks under their policies — see Adsterra's privacy policy and Amazon's privacy notice. Our cookie page lists everything in one table.

Payments

UPI and PayPal payments happen entirely inside your payment app. We only ever see what those apps show a receiver (amount, sender name, your note). We store nothing about it on this site.
