# memory/earnings.md — everything the farm learned about earning. Grounded only, no guru talk.

## 1. The only math that matters
- EV (expected value) decides everything. Positive EV + volume = money. Negative EV (betting, lottery) = slow drain. Farm plays positive-EV only.
- RPM = revenue per 1,000 views. Traffic × RPM ÷ 1000 = ad income. Small RPM × big free traffic still wins when cost is zero.
- Conversion = clicks ÷ views; EPC = earnings per click. Affiliate income = clicks × conversion × commission.
- Rule: $0 cost means ANY payout is infinite ROI. Small money + zero work > big money + work.

## 2. Ad models (what the letters mean)
- CPM: paid per 1,000 impressions. Needs volume. Ours: Adsterra display, JuicyAds banners/pop.
- CPC: paid per click. Needs intent. Search/comparison pages win.
- CPA/CPL/CPI: paid per action/lead/install. Highest per-event, needs trust + targeting.
- Rev-share: % of what advertiser spends on your inventory (JuicyAds). Grows with your traffic quality.

## 9. Risks that kill farms
- Betting/gambling: negative EV + legal risk + frozen accounts. NEVER.
- Manual fulfillment: scales with human hours. NEVER.
- Fake traffic/click fraud: networks ban + claw back. NEVER.
- Secrets in repo: rotate + gitignore. Verified live, never pasted twice.
